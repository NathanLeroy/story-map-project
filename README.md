This storymap uses data from Open Data Philly:

Neighborhood Food Retail:<br>
https://opendataphilly.org/datasets/neighborhood-food-retail/<br>
This dataset looks at the neighborhood availability of "high produce supply stores", e.g. supermarkets, farmers' markets, and produce stores.

Urban Agriculture Projects:<br>
https://opendataphilly.org/datasets/urban-agriculture-projects/<br>
This is a dataset of urban agriculture projects spread throughout the city, including community gardens, orchards, vegetable farms, greenhouses, and youth education gardens.
