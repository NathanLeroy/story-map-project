Philadelphia Urban Agriculture

Growing Food in the City

Farm Philly, the urban agriculture program of Philadelhpia Parks & Recreation, was started in 2014, aiming to give community members access to organic produce, educate the youth on agricultural practices, and promote community engagement. Projects sprinkled around the city include public vegetable farms, orchards, and greenhouses.

Spatial Analysis

Urban Agriculture and Food-Retail Access

The map answers the question: where are urban agriculture projects located relative to neighborhood-level supermarket access?

The map overlays urban agriculture projects with areas classified by supermarket access. Green areas indicate supermarket access, while red areas indicate areas without supermarket access.

Low-income areas such as Kensington, which often lack supermarkets, tend to be underserved by the same community gardens that they would most benefit from.

In the future, when selecting future areas to build urban agriculture projects, we should prioritize low-income, underserved communities.
